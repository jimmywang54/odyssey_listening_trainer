AUDIO
The app deliberately does NOT use phonetic respellings such as “SUR-see”.
It asks iOS/Safari for the best available English voice, preferring Apple Premium/Enhanced voices where installed.
For the most natural iPhone result, install an Enhanced/Premium English voice in iOS Accessibility / Spoken Content settings. Browser speech synthesis cannot guarantee an identical voice on every device. The app uses slower pacing early and increasingly natural pacing later.
